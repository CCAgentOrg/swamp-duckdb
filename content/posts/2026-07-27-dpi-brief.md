## Nilekani Task Force to Overhaul India's Examination System (L1, L6)

Prime Minister Narendra Modi announced a high-powered task force headed by Aadhaar architect and Infosys co-founder Nandan Nilekani to overhaul India's examination system. The move comes after the NEET 2026 paper leak controversy — where a pre-circulated guess paper overlapped with the actual question paper — triggered nationwide "Cockroach Janta Party" protests and the resignation of Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan. The multidisciplinary task force includes experts from technology, space, education, security, and public administration, with a mandate to leverage technology to strengthen the National Testing Agency (NTA). The government is set to table the [Public Examinations (Prevention of Unfair Means) Amendment Bill, 2026](https://swarajyamag.com/news-brief/pm-modi-appoints-nandan-nilekani-to-lead-high-powered-task-force-for-nta-exam-reforms-following-neet-controversy) in Parliament on July 27. Nilekani's track record building DPI — Aadhaar, UPI — signals a technology-first approach to exam integrity.

## Census 2027 Goes Digital: Self-Enumeration Now Live (L1, L3)

India's Census is going digital for the first time. [The Hindu reports](https://www.thehindu.com/videos/watch-census-2027-how-tamil-nadu-residents-can-complete-self-enumeration-online/article71269409.ece) that households in Tamil Nadu can now complete the self-enumeration process online before officials visit. The process involves mobile number verification via OTP, geo-mapping the household location on a map, answering the questionnaire digitally, and receiving a Self-Enumeration ID. This is the first time India's census — which has been paper-based since 1871 — incorporates a digital-first self-service layer, leveraging the mobile and identity infrastructure built over the past decade.

## RuPay-on-UPI: Credit Card Usage Surges, Monetisation Gaps Persist (L2)

A Mint analysis highlights that while [RuPay credit cards linked to UPI](https://www.livemint.com/industry/banking/rupay-credit-cards-upi-monetization-banks-fintechs-credit-card-usage/amp-11784887621642.html) have boosted card usage volumes, profitability remains elusive for issuers. The low-value transaction base that makes UPI attractive to consumers complicates the commercial math for banks and fintechs. Meanwhile, UPI's international expansion continues — NPCI International Payments (NIPL) has linked UPI with payment systems across multiple countries including France, Mauritius, Qatar, UAE, Greece, and Bhutan, enabling both P2P remittances and P2M transactions for Indian travellers abroad.

## Finance Minister Says Budget Has Buffers for Global Shocks (L2, L7)

[Finance Minister Nirmala Sitharaman stated](https://m.economictimes.com/news/economy/policy/budget-has-buffers-to-tackle-global-risks-no-need-to-revisit-its-budget-estimates-yet-says-sitharaman/articleshow/132644018.cms) there is no immediate need to revise Union Budget numbers despite risks from erratic monsoons and elevated global oil prices. Speaking at the Global Fintech Fest context, she reiterated that the government has set aside sufficient fiscal buffers for external shocks — a signal relevant to the DPI ecosystem where public investment in digital infrastructure remains a key growth driver.
